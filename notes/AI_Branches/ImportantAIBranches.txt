Traditional AI vs Generative AI

Traditional AI generates the more significant portion of business value today.

Examples:

- Financial institutions use machine learning to detect fraud and predict a customer's likelihood of repaying loans.
- Insurance companies use machine learning to improve pricing accuracy.
- Retail companies use machine learning to predict demand and optimize inventory.
- E-commerce companies use machine learning to optimize pricing, conversions, and product recommendations.

Traditional AI focuses on analyzing existing data, making predictions, classifications, and recommendations.

Generative AI focuses on creating new content such as text, images, video, audio, code, and 3D models.
