# AI Branches — Summary

## What This Section Covers

This section introduces major branches of artificial intelligence and the
different kinds of problems they solve.

## File Guide

- `ImportantAIBranches.md` — Overview of the main AI specializations.
- `ComputerVision.md` — Extracting meaning from images and video.
- `NLP.md` — Processing, understanding, and generating human language.
- `Robotics.md` — Combining perception, planning, and physical action.
- `GenerativeAI.md` — Creating new text, images, audio, or other content.

## Key Takeaways

- AI is an umbrella field containing several overlapping specializations.
- Computer vision focuses on visual data; NLP focuses on language.
- Robotics connects intelligent software to sensors and physical actions.
- Generative AI learns patterns that allow it to produce new content.
- Real applications often combine more than one branch.
