# Robotics

> **Key takeaway:** Robotics integrates hardware, sensing, control, and AI so a
> machine can perceive its environment, plan behavior, and take physical action.

## Definition

Robotics studies machines that sense and act in the physical world.

## Disciplines Involved

- Mechanical Engineering
- Electrical Engineering
- AI Engineering

## Key Technologies

- Computer Vision
- Reinforcement Learning
- NLP
- SLAM

## Applications

- Tesla Optimus
- Warehouse Automation
- Medical Robotics
