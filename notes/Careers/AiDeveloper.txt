# AI Developer

## AI Developer vs AI Engineer

AI Developer builds foundation AI models.

AI Engineer builds applications on top of foundation models.

Examples of foundation models include:

* GPT
* Gemini
* BERT
* Llama
* Claude

Building foundation models requires significant financial resources and technical expertise.

---

## The Goal of an AI Developer

Organizations that build foundation models must innovate and compete in a rapidly evolving AI landscape.

As a result, AI developers spend a significant amount of time researching new techniques and pushing the boundaries of AI capabilities.

Research and development (R&D) is a core part of the role.

---

## Model Design

AI developers help determine:

* The optimal architecture
* The model design
* The type of neural network
* The number of layers and model depth

These decisions influence the capabilities and performance of the model.

---

## Dataset Engineering

Once the model design is complete, AI developers begin dataset engineering.

This includes:

* Collecting data
* Cleaning data
* Structuring data

These preparations are necessary before pre-training can begin.

---

## Pre-Training

The model is trained on a large corpus of raw data.

Training is expensive and requires careful execution because mistakes can be costly.

---

## Evaluation and Refinement

After training, AI developers:

* Conduct preliminary evaluations
* Refine the model using higher-quality data
* Use prompt engineering
* Continue evaluating and improving the model

This process continues until the model is ready for production.

---

AI developers require strong technical skills, including:

### Programming

* Python
* NumPy
* Pandas

### Mathematics

* Statistics
* Linear Algebra
* Calculus
* Probability

### AI Knowledge

* Deep Learning
* Transformers
* NLP
* Semantic Similarity
* Vector Embeddings
* Tokenization

### Infrastructure

* Docker
* DevOps Practices
* API Design
* Cloud Software

---

## Career Outlook

AI development is one of the most demanding roles in technology.

Relatively few people possess the skills required to build foundation models.

AI engineering is slightly less demanding but is becoming one of the fastest-growing careers in technology.
