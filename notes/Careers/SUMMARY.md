# AI Careers — Summary

## What This Section Covers

This section distinguishes three roles that contribute to AI products from
different technical and organizational perspectives.

## File Guide

- `AIEngineer.md` — Builds and operates AI systems and infrastructure.
- `AiDeveloper.md` — Implements AI-powered application features.
- `AIStrategistRole.md` — Connects AI opportunities to business goals.

## Quick Comparison

| Role | Primary focus | Typical questions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI engineer | Models, data, infrastructure, reliability | How should the system work and scale? |
| AI developer | Product and application integration | How should users interact with AI? |
| AI strategist | Business value, adoption, and risk | Where should AI be used and why? |

## Key Takeaway

The boundaries vary by organization. Strong AI work requires collaboration
among technical implementation, product delivery, and business strategy.
