# AI Strategist Role

## What Is an AI Strategist?

An AI Strategist focuses on overall business strategy and company goals.

A key principle is that an AI strategy must be aligned with the firm's broader business strategy in order to be successful.

---

## How AI Can Benefit a Business

AI can help a business by:

* Selling better products and services
* Improving company processes
* Facilitating decision making

---

## Responsibilities of an AI Strategist

An AI Strategist identifies and maps AI use cases that align with the company's business strategy.

The company then pursues the use cases that are expected to have the highest impact.

Despite its non-technical sounding title, AI Strategist is a technical role.

Key responsibilities include:

* Identifying viable AI use cases
* Helping execute AI initiatives
* Determining the optimal type of AI model
* Comparing performance and costs of different solutions
* Planning production deployment
* Integrating AI into existing products
* Evaluating AI performance
* Optimizing deployed AI systems

---

## AI Evangelism

Another important responsibility is AI evangelism.

This involves:

* Promoting AI adoption throughout the company
* Helping employees understand AI
* Demonstrating how AI can improve competitiveness

---

## Typical Background

This is a relatively new role and requirements continue to evolve.

In many cases, an AI Strategist has a background as a:

* Data Scientist
* Machine Learning Professional
* AI Professional

They typically possess extensive experience in:

* Data
* Machine Learning
* AI

They have also worked closely with business stakeholders and understand what drives business value.

---

## Required Skills

An AI Strategist should possess:

### Technical Skills

* Data expertise
* Machine Learning expertise
* AI expertise
* Development experience

### Business Skills

* Business understanding
* Strategic thinking
* Understanding of project impact

### Soft Skills

* Communication
* Project Management
* Stakeholder Management

---

## Career Level

AI Strategists are often hired into senior leadership positions such as:

* Director
* Head of AI
* Vice President

In some organizations, they may even hold C-suite positions.

Because individuals with this combination of technical, business, and leadership skills are rare, organizations are often willing to compensate them exceptionally well.

---

## Consultants vs Full-Time Roles

Some organizations hire AI Strategists as consultants rather than full-time employees.

### Advantages

* Lower cost
* Exposure to industry best practices
* Experience across multiple companies

### Drawbacks

* Less time to understand the business deeply
* AI strategy may become a one-time project rather than an ongoing initiative
