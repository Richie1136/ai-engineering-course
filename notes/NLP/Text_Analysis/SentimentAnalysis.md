# Sentiment Analysis


Sentiment analysis is a popular Natural Language Processing (NLP) technique used to determine the underlying emotional tone of text. It classifies text as **positive**, **negative**, or **neutral**.


Sentiment analysis is useful when you want to quickly understand how people feel about a particular topic, such as a brand, product, service, movie, or person. It is commonly applied to social media posts, customer reviews, surveys, and news articles.

## Positive Sentiment

Examples include:
 - Expression of Liking Something
 - Enjoyment
 - Positive Emotion

**Example:** 

> "The music in the cafe made me feel really calm and relaxed."

---

## Negative Sentiment 

Expression of Negative Emotion

**Example:**

> "The smell was really bad and it made me feel sick."

---

## Neutral Sentiment

Sentence doesn't really appear to be positive or negative, it sits somewhere in the middle.

**Example:**

> "It was raining."

---


## Key Takeaways

- Sentiment analysis classifies text as positive, negative, or neutral.
- It helps organizations understand opinions expressed in text.
