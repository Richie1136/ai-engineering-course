# Future Directions in NLP — Summary

## File Guide

- `DeepLearningAndNLP.md` — How neural networks learn language representations.
- `NonEnglishNLP.md` — Multilingual data, tokenization, and evaluation concerns.
- `FutureOfNLP.md` — Emerging capabilities and possible applications.

## Key Takeaways

- Deep learning reduces dependence on manually designed text features by
  learning representations from data.
- Performance in English does not guarantee equivalent multilingual quality.
- Language coverage depends on training data, tokenizer design, cultural
  context, and evaluation by relevant speakers.
- Future progress should be assessed alongside fairness, privacy, energy use,
  misinformation, accessibility, and human oversight.
