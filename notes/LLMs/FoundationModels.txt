# Foundation Models

## Importance of Foundation Models

Until recently, machine learning models developed by companies were limited to narrow applications.

Examples:

* Image Recognition models identified objects within images.
* Speech Recognition systems converted spoken language into written text.
* NLP (Natural Language Processing) models handled tasks such as sentiment analysis and translation.
* Time series models predicted stock prices, market volatility, and related outcomes.

These models excelled within a narrow range of tasks.

---

## The Rise of Large Language Models

The introduction of Large Language Models (LLMs) led to a significant transition.

LLMs have complex and sophisticated structures and are trained on massive amounts of data.

Unlike earlier machine learning models, their capabilities are not confined to one or two specific tasks or domains.

With fine-tuning and prompt engineering, LLMs can perform well across many different fields.

---

## From Text Models to Multimodal Systems

Initially, LLMs were text-to-text models that generated responses from textual input.

Over time, they expanded their capabilities and could work with:

* Code
* Excel files
* PDF files
* Images
* Videos

The transition from text-only systems to models capable of working with text, code, images, and video marked an important shift.

---

## Foundation Models

The AI community uses the term Foundation Models to describe these systems.

The name comes from the idea that these models serve as a foundational layer for applications across many disciplines.

Characteristics:

* Enormous in size
* Capable of performing many different tasks
* Extremely powerful
* Support a wide range of applications

---

## The Future of Foundation Models

Big technology companies want to compete in the foundation model market.

Several governments may also seek to create their own foundation models.

Other organizations may:

* Build application-specific in-house models.
* Build on top of existing foundation models.
* Use foundation models created by the open-source community.
