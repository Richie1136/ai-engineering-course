# Deep Learning Recap

## Introduction

Modern Artificial Intelligence has reached the point where computers can perform tasks that once seemed impossible.

For example, we can show a machine a picture of a flower and ask it to identify the species. In Natural Language Processing (NLP), we can build generative models capable of having conversations about flowers, discussing their characteristics, answering questions, and even identifying them based on a textual description.

These advancements raise an important question:

> **What led to the development of such powerful AI models?**

---

## Transformers

Large Language Models (LLMs) are built upon a neural network architecture called the **Transformer**.

Transformers were introduced by researchers at Google Brain in 2017 and have become the foundation of modern NLP systems, including models such as ChatGPT.

Transformers are a specialized architecture within the broader field of **deep learning**.

---

## What is Deep Learning?

Deep learning is a type of machine learning that uses artificial neural networks to discover patterns in complex data.

A neural network begins by receiving input data, such as:

- Text
- Images
- Audio
- Numerical data

The input passes through multiple interconnected layers of neurons.

Each neuron performs mathematical operations and passes information to the next layer.

The connections between neurons have associated **weights**, which determine how much influence each neuron has on the final prediction.

During training, these weights are continuously adjusted to reduce the difference between the model's prediction and the correct answer. This optimization process allows the model to gradually improve its performance over time.

As the model learns from increasingly large amounts of data, it becomes capable of solving complex tasks such as:

- Text generation
- Image recognition
- Text classification
- Language translation
- Question answering
- Decision making
- Problem solving

## Key Takeaways

- Deep learning uses artificial neural networks to learn patterns from data.
- Neural networks improve by adjusting their weights during training.
- Transformers are a specialized deep learning architecture.
- Modern Large Language Models are built using Transformer architectures.
- These advances have enabled AI systems to perform sophisticated Natural Language Processing tasks such as text generation, translation, summarization, and question answering.
