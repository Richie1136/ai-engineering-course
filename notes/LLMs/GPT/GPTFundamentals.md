# GPT Fundamentals

ChatGPT has taken the field of Natural Language Processing (NLP) by storm with its ability to generate content, write code, have conversations, and much more.

ChatGPT is a **state-of-the-art Large Language Model (LLM)** developed by **OpenAI**.

The **GPT** in ChatGPT stands for **Generative Pre-trained Transformer**.

---

# What Does GPT Stand For?

## Generative

We say the model is **generative** because it can generate human-like text given a prompt or context.

ChatGPT can continue text in a coherent and contextually appropriate manner, making it useful for tasks such as:

- Text completion
- Dialogue generation
- Content creation

---

## Pre-trained

The **pre-trained** aspect of GPT is crucial before fine-tuning for specific tasks.

GPT undergoes a pre-training phase where it learns from massive amounts of text data.

This process equips the model with:

- A deep understanding of language
- Grammar
- A broad vocabulary

---

## Transformer

The **Transformer** in GPT refers to the Transformer architecture discussed previously.

# Evolution of GPT Models

The development of GPT models represents a remarkable journey in the evolution of Large Language Models (LLMs), marked by substantial increases in scale, performance, capabilities, and real-world applicability.

---

## GPT-1 (2018)

GPT-1 emerged in **2018** as OpenAI's first general-purpose text generation model.

- Trained on approximately **40 GB** of internet text.
- **117 million parameters.**
- Demonstrated text completion and generation capabilities.
- Had limitations in understanding long-range context and generating coherent long-form text.

---

## GPT-2 (2019)

GPT-2 was released in **2019**.

Major improvements included:

- **1.5 billion parameters.**
- Larger and more diverse training dataset.
- Much stronger text generation abilities.
- Improved training objective and text sampling techniques.

---

## GPT-3 (2020)

GPT-3 was released in **2020**.

Major improvements included:

- **175 billion parameters.**
- Trained on a massive internet-scale dataset.
- Introduced powerful **few-shot learning** capabilities.
- Improved training methodology (GShard).

---

## GPT-3.5 (2022)

GPT-3.5 was released in **2022**.

Major improvements included:

- Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (**RLHF**).
- Reduced toxic and harmful outputs.
- Better conversational abilities.
- Served as the foundation for the original ChatGPT.

---

## GPT-4 (2023)

GPT-4 was released in **2023**.

Major improvements included:

- Significantly stronger reasoning capabilities.
- Multimodal support, allowing both text and image inputs.
- Improved reliability and factual accuracy.
- Continued use of RLHF during training.

---

## GPT-4o (2024)

GPT-4o ("o" stands for **omni**) was released in **2024**.

Major improvements included:

- Native multimodal capabilities across text, images, and audio.
- Faster response times.
- Lower operating costs.
- More natural real-time conversations.

# ChatGPT

ChatGPT is a version of a GPT model that has been specifically fine-tuned for conversational interactions.

It is optimized to provide more natural and engaging responses in a chat or dialogue format.

ChatGPT has undergone additional fine-tuning to make it more suitable for chatbot and virtual assistant applications.

OpenAI also provides an API that allows developers to integrate GPT models into their own applications and build custom AI-powered solutions.

---

# Summary

GPT stands for **Generative Pre-trained Transformer**.

GPT models:

- Generate human-like text.
- Are pre-trained on massive text datasets.
- Use the Transformer architecture.
- Can be fine-tuned for specific tasks.
- Have evolved from GPT-1 to GPT-5 with major improvements in reasoning, multimodal understanding, and conversational abilities.
