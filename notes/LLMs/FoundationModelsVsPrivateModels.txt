# Foundation Models vs Private Models

## Build vs Buy

This decision determines whether to outsource specific company processes or retain them internally.

The golden rule MBA graduates are taught is that a company should try to retain internally all strategic value-adding activities because they are essential for business.

Management can externally delegate non-core activities aimed at efficiency.

---

## Building Foundation Models

Few companies and organizations worldwide will be able to build their own foundation models.

Creating foundation models is costly and requires significant scale.

Most companies face a critical choice:

* Build on an existing foundation model.
* Develop their own version from an open-source model.

---

## Using Existing Foundation Models

In many cases, firms lack the expertise and resources required to train a custom model.

However, they still recognize AI as a core value-adding component of their business.

As a result, many companies rely on organizations such as OpenAI that provide access to foundation models through a model-as-a-service arrangement.

---

## The Real Competitive Advantage

AI can be a core value-generating aspect of a business, but AI availability and development do not fully depend on a company's in-house efforts.

The true differentiating factor is the ability to adapt AI to specific use cases.

Key areas include:

* Prompt Engineering
* Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
* Fine-Tuning

The expertise of in-house AI engineers in these areas can help businesses gain an edge over competitors.

---

## Future Outlook

The demand for skilled AI engineers is expected to surge in the near future.

These developments represent a significant paradigm shift that is likely to continue.
