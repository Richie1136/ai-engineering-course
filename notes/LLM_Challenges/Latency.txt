# Latency

## Why Latency Matters

One key issue when building customer-facing applications is that today's users have low tolerance for slow loading times.

The latency issue becomes even more critical when business customers use AI to boost productivity or build products based on AI models.

In these situations:

* Delays frustrate users.
* Business operations are impacted.
* Revenue can be affected.

---

## The Challenge with LLMs

The biggest latency challenge for LLMs today is that they use an autoregressive architecture.

In an autoregressive model, each word generated depends on the words that came before it.

Because of this sequential process, each step must wait for the previous step to complete before generating the next word.

---

## Example

Consider the sentence:

"My favorite sport is basketball."

If an LLM generated each word sequentially and required 0.2 seconds per word, it would take approximately one second to generate the complete sentence.

---

## Possible Solutions

AI developers are exploring several approaches to reduce latency:

* Alternative model architectures
* Parallel computing
* Model optimization

---

## Smaller Models

One effective immediate strategy is to optimize model size.

Smaller models are often faster than larger, more complex models.

As a result, reducing model size can improve response speed and help create a better user experience.
