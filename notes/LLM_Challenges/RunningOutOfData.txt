# Running Out of Data

## The Problem

If GPT-4 has already read and processed a significant portion of the publicly available data on the internet, where will the training data for future models come from?

AI developers face the risk of running out of high-quality training data, which could slow the growth of model capabilities.

---

## AI-Generated Content

As AI tools become more popular, a growing percentage of online content may be generated by AI.

This creates a challenge for future LLMs because they may increasingly encounter:

* Repetitive content
* Derivative content
* AI-generated content based on previous AI-generated content

As a result, future models may simply repeat information produced by earlier models.

Potential consequences include:

* Increased hallucinations
* Increased biases
* Reduced accuracy

---

## Data Access Challenges

Several large organizations have filed lawsuits and restricted data scraping of their websites.

Platforms such as Reddit and Quora changed their policies to make it illegal for AI developers to scrape platform data.

These restrictions reduce the amount of publicly available training data that can be collected.

---

## Content Licensing

To address this challenge, OpenAI has launched a content licensing program.

OpenAI has signed agreements with organizations that own large proprietary datasets.

Examples include:

* Shutterstock
* Axel Springer
* The Associated Press
* Le Monde
* Prisa Media

OpenAI reportedly offered publishers between $1 million and $5 million per year for access to archives used to train generative AI models.

---

## Future Outlook

Competition among companies building LLMs is likely to increase demand for large proprietary datasets.

As a result, the value and cost of high-quality proprietary data may continue to rise in the future.
