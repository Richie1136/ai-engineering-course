# Hallucinations and Inconsistencies

## AI Is Not Always Correct

ChatGPT is brilliant, but sometimes we give it more credit than it deserves.

We can make big mistakes if we do not check an AI's output.

As a rule of thumb, an expert in their field works with AI and reasons within the AI conversation rather than blindly trusting every answer.

OpenAI clearly states:

"ChatGPT can make mistakes. Check important info."

---

## Two Types of Undesired Behavior

Generative AI tools can exhibit two major types of undesired behavior:

1. Hallucinations
2. Inconsistencies

---

## Hallucinations

Hallucinations occur when an AI model provides false output while generating a response.

Generative AI models often predict the next word in a sequence using context to improve accuracy.

Despite their sophistication, these predictions can sometimes be incorrect.

Another cause of hallucinations is that the model may have been trained on factually incorrect data.

### Possible Mitigation

One prompt engineering technique is to instruct the model:

"Provide an answer only if you know the answer."

In some situations, this can help reduce hallucinations.

---

## Inconsistencies

Inconsistencies occur when a model provides very different responses to the same question.

When ChatGPT first became popular, users often felt that the AI appeared smarter in some sessions and less capable in others.

This can occur because of variations in the hardware used to run the model or other uncontrollable factors, especially when the model is hosted externally.

### Possible Mitigation

One unproven approach is to instruct the AI to take its time before responding.

The idea is that the system may avoid rushing and potentially allocate more resources to generating the response.

---

## Future Research

Detecting hallucinations and inconsistencies and finding ways to address them will be one of the major topics of AI research in the years ahead.

This area is expected to become a significant market as organizations continue to adopt AI systems.
