# Looking Ahead — Summary

## What This Section Covers

This section considers the future impact of AI and the ethical responsibilities
that accompany increasingly capable systems.

## File Guide

- `FutureOfAI.md` — Possible directions for AI capability and adoption.
- `AIEthics.md` — Fairness, privacy, safety, accountability, and responsible use.

## Key Takeaways

- Future capability should be considered alongside real-world consequences.
- Ethical AI requires attention throughout design, data collection, deployment,
  and monitoring—not only after a problem occurs.
- Important concerns include bias, transparency, privacy, misuse, safety,
  human oversight, and accountability.
- Responsible decisions depend on the application context and affected people.
