# Future of AI

## AI Market Growth

Bloomberg reports that by 2032, the generative AI market could reach $1.3 trillion.

For comparison, the annual GDP of the United States is approximately $25 trillion.

PricewaterhouseCoopers (PwC) is even more optimistic, predicting that the AI market could reach $15.7 trillion by 2030.

Although estimates differ, it is widely expected that the AI sector will experience significant growth.

---

## Three Critical Resources

The future growth of AI depends on three essential resources:

* Data
* Electricity
* Computing Power

The availability and cost of these resources will influence the pace of AI development.

---

## The Data Challenge

The availability of training data is becoming an increasingly important issue.

Training AI models on:

* Social media content
* Proprietary website data
* User-generated content

can create ethical and legal concerns.

As foundation models continue to grow, organizations may need to secure additional data through licensing agreements.

Future possibilities include:

* More content licensing deals
* Restrictions on training with private data
* User compensation for access to personal data

---

## AI-Generated Content

As AI-generated content becomes more common on the internet, developers face new challenges.

If future AI models are trained on large amounts of AI-generated content, potential issues include:

* Feedback loops
* Reduced accuracy
* Increased bias

This may become an important area of future research.

---

## Electricity Demand

Large language models require substantial amounts of electricity.

As AI adoption increases globally, demand for AI services and computing infrastructure is expected to grow significantly.

Important considerations include:

* Energy consumption
* Carbon footprint
* Impact on energy prices
* Electrical grid capacity

AI may play an important role in shaping future energy markets.

---

## Computing Power

Developing and operating advanced AI models requires powerful computing hardware.

High-performance AI chips have become a critical resource for AI development.

The rapid growth of AI increased demand for semiconductors and positioned companies such as Nvidia to benefit from this trend.

Although shortages currently exist, competition among semiconductor manufacturers may increase future supply.

---

## Open Source vs Closed Source

Another major question is whether open source AI models will become competitive with proprietary models.

Possible outcomes include:

* Open source models becoming increasingly competitive
* Large technology companies continuing to dominate the market
* A combination of both approaches coexisting

The future balance between open source and closed source AI remains uncertain.

---

## Looking Ahead

Regardless of which organizations ultimately lead the market, AI is expected to have a transformative impact on society.

The influence of AI may rival or even surpass the changes created by the internet and the broader digital revolution.
