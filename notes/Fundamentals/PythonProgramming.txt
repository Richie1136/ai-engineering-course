# Python Programming for AI

## No-Code and Low-Code Tools

No-code and low-code tools are a popular way to get started with AI.

They have grown in popularity in recent years and can be used to:

* Develop chatbots
* Explore AI product ideas
* Build certain real-world applications

These tools can provide an initial understanding of how AI products are created.

---

## Why Coding Matters

If you are serious about learning AI and developing AI-powered applications, you must know how to code.

Learning to code is essential because it allows you to engage with AI at a much deeper level.

Coding enables developers to:

* Use APIs from major AI providers
* Tailor AI behavior through prompt engineering
* Integrate databases
* Modify model parameters

These capabilities are crucial for creating sophisticated AI-driven applications and are currently beyond the reach of many no-code tools.

---

## Major AI Providers

Coding allows developers to work directly with AI providers such as:

* OpenAI
* Llama
* Anthropic

---

## Why Python?

Python is the leading programming language for data science and AI.

Its comprehensive libraries and robust infrastructure enhance scalability for AI engineers and developers.
